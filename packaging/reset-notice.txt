Setup installs Hyper GPU Support, its helper tools, licence notices and a Start menu shortcut.

Use the app to configure GPU support for your existing Hyper-V virtual machines.

Close Hyper GPU Support before continuing. Installing or updating backs up protected app data, then clears saved app settings, profiles, history and remembered VM sign-in details. Your VMs and guest files are unchanged.

Start Setup normally and approve the Windows administrator prompt when asked.
